02 — CompetitionsRoundwise Details
01 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 1 – AI Poster Design

Round 1 (30 min): Create a poster on the given theme using Gemini AI, ChatGPT, or any other AI tool. Theme provided on the spot.

Judging: Creativity + Design Appeal + AI Tool Usage.

02 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 2 – Best Prompt Challenge

Round 1 (30 min): Write a problem-solving prompt using ChatGPT or any other AI Tool. Problem statement given on the spot.

Judging: Clarity + Innovation + Usefulness of Results.

03 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 3 – AI Meme Creation

Round 1 (30 min): Create an engaging meme on the topic given on the spot using Gemini AI or image generative tools.

Judging: Humor + Originality.

04 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 4 – AI Presentation (1 Hour)

Round 1 (30 min): Create a PPT presentation based on the given topic using Gamma, Canva AI, or any other AI tool.

Judging: Structure + Creativity + AI Use.

05 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 5 – AI Story Creation

Round 1 (30 min): Write a short story with ChatGPT or any AI tool on the theme given on the spot.

Judging: Storytelling + Imagination.

06 30 Min | 1 Member

Event 6 – AI Voice Acting

Round 1 (30 min): Generate a voice greeting of 30 to 60 seconds (birthday/festival wish) using ElevenLabs, Narakeet, etc. Theme on spot.

Judging: Creativity + Fun Impact.

07 4 Rounds | 1 Member

Event 7 – AI Quiz (4 Rounds)

Mastery quiz on tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, MidJourney, Canva AI, Gamma.app, ElevenLabs, Runway ML, Notion AI, Copilot, etc.

Round 1 – Basics of AI (30 min) 30 MCQ questions; mark correct answers.
Round 2 – True/False & Identify (30 min) True/False & Guess AI-generated photos.
Round 3 – Guess the Tool (30 min) Guess the AI tool name from questions.
Round 4 – Rapid Fire (30 min) Rapid questions based on AI tools.
